03Our mission

A marketing studio for
owner-run businesses

Market & Matter exists for the business where the person who signs off the spend also answers the emails. Most marketing treats that person as a lead to be worked. We treat them as the buyer, and we built the whole company around the fact that they decide.

We think a small business knows more about what it needs than most agencies give it credit for. It knows its customers, its margins and its busy months. What it usually lacks is a straight way to buy the help.

So we decided what the work is before anyone asks. Each service is a fixed piece of work with a fixed scope, which means an owner can weigh it against everything else competing for the same money.

We would rather be chosen than persuaded. If a service is right for you, the page should be enough. If it is not, better you find that out on the page than three months in.
